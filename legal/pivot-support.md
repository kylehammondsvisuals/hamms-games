# PIVOT! Support

PIVOT! is a co-op moving-day comedy by HAMMS Studios. You inherit Grandpa Gus's tiny moving company (and Earl, who came with it), name the business, and pack couches, pianos, fish tanks and a whole lot of boxes into the truck before the clock runs out. Level up, buy bigger trucks, hire a crew and move into a downtown tower.

## Controls

**iPhone and iPad:** put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side of the screen and slide to walk (push to the edge to run). Drag anywhere on the right side to look around. GRAB picks things up and DROP sets them down. JUMP, DRIVE (at the truck cab, to drive a full load to the new house) and CREW LIFT (calls a crew member to grab the other end of something heavy). HAUL, FOLLOW, PUT DOWN and HORN give the whole crew orders. II pauses.

**Controllers:** PlayStation and Xbox controllers work on iPhone, iPad and PC.

**PC keyboard and mouse:** WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to grab or drop, Space to jump, F to drive the truck, 1-4 for crew orders, H for the horn, Esc to pause.

## Your save

The game saves after every job and every purchase. Saves stay on your device; deleting the app deletes your save.
