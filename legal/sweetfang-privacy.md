# Sweetfang Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

Sweetfang is a single-player game made by HAMMS Studios. Short version: **Sweetfang doesn't collect, send or share any of your information.**

## What the game keeps

Your saved game (where Mochi is, the Ember Oil and Sugar you've found, your outfits and unlocked moves) and your settings (volume, controls) are stored **only on your own device**. They never leave it, and HAMMS Studios can't see them.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no sign-in and no online features.
- No advertising and no ad tracking.
- No analytics, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
- We never sell, rent or share any information, because we don't have any.

## Kids and families

Sweetfang is family friendly. It has no chat, no other players and no way to share personal details.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your saved game and settings from your device.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top.
