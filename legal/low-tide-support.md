# LOW TIDE Support

LOW TIDE is a 1970s harbor crime story by HAMMS Studios. Ray Castell owes the Moretti crew fifty thousand dollars and has three days to pay: run cargo past the Harbor Patrol, take side work on the docks, steal a car when you need one and buy guns from Augie under the counter.

## Controls

**iPhone and iPad:** put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side of the screen and slide to walk (push to the rim to run) or to steer. Drag on open screen to turn the camera. On foot: drag the AIM pad to aim and push it out to fire (a quick tap fires one shot; with a Molotov or grenade, drag to see the arc and let go to throw), JUMP, RELOAD (it also says HOTWIRE, SEARCH, LOAD and so on when there's something to use), GUN (tap for the next gun, hold and slide for the weapon wheel), CROUCH and LIGHT. By a car or the boat a GET IN button appears. Driving: GAS, BRAKE (REVERSE on the boat), HANDBRAKE (LAMP on the boat), HORN and LIGHTS. II pauses. In a story scene tap for the next line, or hold SKIP.

**Controllers:** PlayStation and Xbox controllers work on iPhone, iPad and PC.

**Graphics:** Settings > Graphics has the quality preset, render scale and 30 or 60 fps. Newer iPhones and iPads can run Medium or High at 60.

## Your save

The game saves at every dawn and after every job. Saves stay on your device; deleting the app deletes your save.
