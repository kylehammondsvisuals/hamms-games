# Brick Workshop Support

Brick Workshop is a building game by HAMMS Studios: follow the instructions to build sets, design your own, race, play laser tag and mini-games, and explore Brick City with friends.

## Get help

Email **kylehammondsmedia@gmail.com** and tell us what happened, what device you play on and your builder name. We usually answer within a couple of days.

## Common questions

**How do I play with friends?**
Open the **Computer** in your workshop, go to **Friends**, and add your friend by their builder name. Once they accept, you can see when they're online and join their game.

**Can I play on iPhone and PC with the same builder?**
Yes. On iPhone, choose **Already play on PC? Sign in with your HAMMS account** when you start, or later from the Computer. Your builder, progress and builds come with you.

**Where are my photos?**
Every photo you take is in the **Photo Book** in the game. On iPhone they're also saved to your Camera Roll (allow photo access when the game asks).

**Voice chat on iPhone**
Hold **TALK** to talk. You can switch to open mic in **Computer > Settings**. The game asks for microphone permission the first time you talk.

**Someone is being mean. What do I do?**
Open the **Computer**, find them in your friends or the online list and choose **REPORT** or **BLOCK**. Blocked players can't message you, talk to you or join you. We review every report and remove players who break the Community Rules.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open the **Computer**, go to **Settings**, scroll to **Your Account** and choose **Delete my account**. This permanently deletes your builder and everything stored on Brick Net. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

## Community Rules

Be kind. Keep it family friendly. Keep personal info private. No cheating or scamming. Report and block anyone who breaks the rules. There's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive players: content that breaks the rules is removed, and so are the players who break them.
