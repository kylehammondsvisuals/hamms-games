# Brick Workshop Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

Brick Workshop is made by HAMMS Studios. This policy explains what the game (on iPhone and PC) collects, why, and how you can delete it. Short version: we only keep what the game needs to work. No ads, no tracking, no selling data, and nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

## What we collect

**Your builder.** When you create a Brick Net account you pick a builder name (a nickname, not your real name). We store that name, your character's look, your game progress (level, studs, unlocks and saves), your friends list and friend requests, and the players you've blocked.

**Things you make.** If you're signed in, your saved builds, custom sets, wall paintings, pictures you hang in the Brick City Gallery and photos you take inside the game are backed up to Brick Net so they're on all your devices. Photos are only ever the in-game pictures you take; the game never reads your phone's photo library. On iPhone, when you take a photo in the game it's also saved to your Camera Roll (you're asked for permission first).

**HAMMS account (optional).** If you choose to sign in with a HAMMS Studios account, your email address and password are sent securely (HTTPS) to the HAMMS sign-in service to check who you are. The game doesn't keep your password.

**Reports.** If you report a player, we keep the report (who reported whom, the reason and the note you typed) so we can review it.

**Chat and voice.** Text chat and voice chat go to the other players in your game in real time and aren't recorded or stored. The microphone is only used while you're talking (on iPhone, while you hold TALK unless you turn on open mic), and only after you allow it.

**Connection details.** Like any online game, our server sees your device's IP address while you're connected so it can send you data. We don't build profiles from it.

## What we don't do

- No advertising and no ad tracking.
- No third-party analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites.
- We never sell or rent your information.

## Kids and families

Brick Workshop is built to be family friendly. Builder names, chat and other text go through a filter, every player can report and block others, and players who break the Community Rules are removed. We don't ask for real names, ages, addresses or phone numbers, and the Community Rules tell players never to share them.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on Brick Net while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the game: open the **Computer**, go to **Settings**, scroll to **Your Account** and choose **Delete account**. That permanently removes your builder, progress, friends, cloud backups, gallery pictures and leaderboard entries from our server. Anything saved only on your device stays there until you delete the app.

## Where it's stored

Brick Net runs on a server in the United States. Data travels to and from it over the internet.

## Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game.
