# LOW TIDE Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

LOW TIDE (on the App Store as "LOW TIDE: Port Hollis") is a single-player game made by HAMMS Studios. Short version: **LOW TIDE doesn't collect, send or share any of your information.**

## What the game keeps

Your saved game (the day you're on, Ray's cash and debt, his look, what he owns and your case file) and your settings (graphics, volume, vibration, subtitles) are stored **only on your own device**. They never leave it, and HAMMS Studios can't see them.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no sign-in and no online features on iPhone or iPad.
- No advertising and no ad tracking.
- No analytics, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
- We never sell, rent or share any information, because we don't have any.

## On PC

The PC version is started through the HAMMS Studios launcher, which signs you in to your HAMMS account. The game itself doesn't send anything anywhere.

## Kids and families

LOW TIDE is a crime story with realistic gun violence and is meant for adults. It has no chat, no other players and no way to share personal details.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your saved game and settings from your device.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top.
