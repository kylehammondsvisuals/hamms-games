# Sweetfang Support

Sweetfang is a sweet-and-sour 3D platformer by HAMMS Studios. Guide Mochi, a little fox-bunny with a monster named FANG hiding inside, across eighteen floating lands to find the nineteen flasks of Ember Oil.

## Controls

**iPhone and iPad:** put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side of the screen and slide to move. The buttons on the right are JUMP (hold to jump higher or glide), ATTACK, SLAM, FANG and USE. USE lights up and shows its job (TALK, REST, OPEN or ENTER) when you're next to a friend, a lantern, a chest or a door. Tap ✕ to close the map, menus and mini-games. Pinch the map, or use its + and − buttons, to zoom.

**Controllers:** PlayStation and Xbox controllers work on iPhone, iPad and PC.

**PC keyboard:** arrows or WASD to move, Space to jump, E to talk or use, M for the map, Esc to pause.

## Your save

The game saves at every lantern and when you leave a land. Saves stay on your device; deleting the app deletes your save.
