# PIVOT! Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

PIVOT! (on the App Store as "PIVOT! Moving Day") is a single-player game made by HAMMS Studios. Short version: **PIVOT! doesn't collect, send or share any of your information.**

## What the game keeps

Your saved game (your company name, cash, trucks, crew, office and stars) and your settings (volume, look sensitivity) are stored **only on your own device**. They never leave it, and HAMMS Studios can't see them.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no sign-in and no online features on iPhone or iPad.
- No advertising and no ad tracking.
- No analytics, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
- We never sell, rent or share any information, because we don't have any.

## On PC

The PC version is started through the HAMMS Studios launcher, which signs you in to your HAMMS account and passes your HAMMS name to the game so it can pre-fill your name. The game itself doesn't send anything anywhere.

## Kids and families

PIVOT! has no chat, no other players and no way to share personal details.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your saved game and settings from your device.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top.
